### Venue and goal

The 2025 edition of the workshop was organized by Guillaume Sérieys and held in Annecy (France) from September 1 to September 3, 2025. 
This inaugural edition aimed to establish the scope and objectives of future editions, to experiment with presentations format, and to foster new collaborations among participants.
Themes of the presentations and discussions were shape analysis, optimal transport, Lie-Poisson reduction, and metric geometry.



### Participants

Participants of the workshop were
- Théo Dumont, Université Gustave Eiffel
- Rayane Mouhli, Université Paris Cité
- Thomas Pierron, ENS Paris-Saclay
- Guillaume Sérieys, Université Paris Cité
